Notice that nothing connects the two backends except the export script, and it only copies one way. The clinical backend holds real patient data and serves the patient and provider apps. The labeling backend holds recordings with the names removed, so outside cardiologists can rate them without ever touching patient records. Because the two never call each other, the outside raters have no path to the patient data, and the labeling side can be opened to them without bringing in the privacy rules that cover the clinical side.
