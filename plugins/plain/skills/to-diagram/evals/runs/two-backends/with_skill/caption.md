We run two separate backends. The clinical one holds real patient data under US health privacy rules (HIPAA) and serves the patient app and the provider portal. The labeling one holds only anonymized heart recordings, which outside cardiologists rate. Notice that the two never call each other: the only link is an export script that copies anonymized recordings from clinical to labeling, in that one direction. Because of this, a rater's account can never reach a patient's identity.
